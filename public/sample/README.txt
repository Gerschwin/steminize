The sample song that comes with Steminize ("Try a sample song" on the welcome screen).

It is original: the guitar, bass and drums are synthesised by tools/make-example-song.py in this repository, and the tab is written
from the same list of notes, so nothing here is sampled from or based on anyone's recording. Same licence as the rest of the
project (MIT). To rebuild it: python3 tools/make-example-song.py --sample public/sample/example-tab
